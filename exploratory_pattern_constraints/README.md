# Exploratory explanation attacks under graph-pattern constraints

Separate new-search pilot: GNNExplainer on 10 fixed randomly sampled targets per dataset, followed by a conditional larger 50-target-per-qualifying-dataset study with two search trials. The 50 expansion targets are disjoint from screening targets. No result is automatically merged into the main study.

Literature motivation: Heijmans et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31718, studies financial multigraph prediction evasion with a short-cycle regularizer. This pilot studies prediction-preserving explanation disruption. Cycle constraints, multigraph attacks and degree-preserving rewiring are prior ideas. Frozen features mean this is a graph-store corruption experiment, not a claim of economically valid transaction manipulation; feature consistency is being tested separately.

Threat model: fixed known classifier, local two-hop clean candidate domain, three distinct existing non-self-loop transaction rows with distinct sources and destinations. Redirect their receivers while preserving each sender. Three removed and three inserted row connections constitute six edge edits (three record rewirings). New connections may be parallel to existing records; self-loops and any cancellation between the three removed/inserted endpoint pairs are prohibited. Rewired transactions receive new explanation identifiers to prevent changed endpoints from being counted as the same explanatory edge.

Three conditions: (1) source-fixed unrestricted receiver reassignment, (2) cyclic receiver permutations preserving every node's in/out degree, (3) condition 2 plus exact preservation of global directed length-2 and length-3 simple-cycle counts, including parallel-edge multiplicity. Self-loops are unchanged and excluded from cycle counts. Counts are global, not restricted to the explainer neighborhood. Preserving totals need not preserve individual motifs or financial semantics.

For each condition, generate at most 40 unique structurally admissible candidates with a cap of 20,000 raw structural draws. Report rejection rate, actual prediction/explainer calls, and shortfalls; do not substitute targets or relax constraints. Evaluate prediction preservation and a maximum 0.02 change in the original class probability. At least ten explanation-input edges are needed. Select the feasible candidate with minimum top-25% endpoint-multiplicity overlap; also evaluate the first structural candidate as a blind random baseline. This is best-of-40 random search, not a new learned attack algorithm. Equal maximum budgets do not imply equal realized calls or equal structural sampling cost.

New selection seed 120001; five unseen paired explanation seeds 121001--121005 and clean-repeat seeds 122001--122005. Use exact row overlap and endpoint-multiplicity overlap, top-10 physical fidelity and label/probability preservation. Missing feasible candidates remain in the denominator with zero joint disruption. Pair matching is a diagnostic under the fixed-feature architecture, not a corrected explanation-accuracy measure. Independent clean repeats are a variability reference, not a perfectly matched null.

Primary screening endpoint: degree+cycle-constrained best-search fresh endpoint-overlap reduction relative to independent clean reruns, multiplied by prediction/probability feasibility. Average seeds within targets. Expansion requires all 10 screening targets, >=80% feasible coverage, mean primary endpoint >=0.10 with 95% target-bootstrap lower bound >0, and mean feasible joint disruption >=0.30. Freeze targets, source hashes, seeds and gate before results. Run expansion only for qualifying datasets, with selection seeds 120002/120003 and fresh seeds 123001--123005/124001--124005. Report phases separately and average trials within targets. Failure to pass is retained as a negative pilot.

Technical checks: 500 random toy multigraph comparisons against independent dense trace counts and cycle enumeration; real edited graphs checked with independent affected-cycle enumeration; full/local model prediction and original native explanation/fidelity parity on one target per dataset. Freeze classifier weights. One CPU thread, below-normal priority and 20% GPU allocator cap. Resume from immutable per-case outputs only when all code/input hashes match.
